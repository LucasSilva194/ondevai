# OndeVai Video Design

## Style Prompt
Warm personal-finance product film, grounded in OndeVai's existing interface. Clear, steady, and reassuring; build from a monthly summary card and real interface details. The light canvas should feel like warm paper, with forest green carrying the main emphasis and terracotta used sparingly.

## Colors
- Background: `#f7f3ea`
- Primary text: `#182d25`
- Brand/accent: `#1b3f33`
- Secondary accent: `#c46f56`
- Surface: `#fcfaf5`

## Typography
- System sans-serif stack, consistent with the app.
- Strong, compact headings; large tabular figures; quiet supporting labels.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use a dark dashboard aesthetic or neon finance colors.
- Do not imply figures are real account data.
- Do not add bank integrations or product claims absent from the source.
- Do not use dense copy or rapid flashing text.
