# Brag Plan: OndeVai

## What is this app?
OndeVai organizes personal expenses, income, budgets, and savings goals into a clear view of your finances.

## The angle
Make personal finance feel legible: one everyday expense becomes a monthly overview, a budget check, and a sense of control. Use a real-looking app flow with the landing page's explicitly illustrative June figures; keep the claims grounded and the visual tone warm rather than bank-like.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The OndeVai mark appears, then the exact line: “Perceba para onde vai o seu dinheiro.” A small expense slips into frame and resolves into an organized monthly view.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The illustrated June dashboard shows “Saldo disponível” at 1 248,60 €, the month chart, expenses of 842,30 €, and income of 2 090,90 €.
- Show a fictional “Café · 3,20 €” entry landing in “Despesas e rendimentos”; keep the amount visibly marked as an illustrative example.
- A budget progress row fills, then settles within its limit, echoing the site's “Orçamentos que fazem sentido” feature.

## Outro / punchline
“Sem ligação a bancos. Sem anúncios. Os seus dados ficam sob o seu controlo.” Land on the OndeVai mark and “O seu dinheiro, explicado.”

## User flow worth showing
Open the monthly view → record an expense → see monthly spending and budget progress. The recording interaction and data are illustrative; no real account or customer information is used.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: warm, calm personal-finance product film
- Interpretation: measured motion and confident holds; useful app details stay large and readable, with a light rhythmic bed and no comic exaggeration.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Clear view — 3s
Warm ivory field; OndeVai mark assembles beside “Perceba para onde vai o seu dinheiro.” A small expense dot moves into a tidy ring. Hold the headline for readability.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the mark arrives, then the dot moves into place.
Audio intent: gentle start, light lift.
Audio-coupled idea: soft entry accent on the mark; no per-character typing.
Music: warm, steady.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The month, at a glance — 4s
Recreate the landing page's illustrative “Este mês” dashboard card: June selector, “Saldo disponível 1 248,60 €”, line chart, expenses 842,30 €, income 2 090,90 €, and “Dentro do orçamento”. Treat the figures as illustrative, not live account data.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the balance settles first, chart draws in, then the two totals appear together and hold.
Audio intent: clear reveal with a restrained lift.
Audio-coupled idea: chart draw and reveal aligned near a strong cue; totals remain together for reading.
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — One small entry — 4s
Show a compact expense form in the app's warm card style. “Café” and “3,20 €” appear as fictional example data; a cursor presses “Guardar”. A saved row joins the month's activity.
Sequential/interaction: yes — type the two values, click save, row arrives.
Audio intent: calm, tactile confirmation.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft click at save; row lands on the beat.
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Keep a limit in view — 4.5s
Show an illustrative category budget row with a forest-green progress bar filling to a comfortable in-limit state, alongside “Orçamentos que fazem sentido” and “Acompanhe como evoluem ao longo do mês.”
Sequential/interaction: yes — category row appears, bar fills, remaining amount settles.
Audio intent: quiet reassurance.
Audio-coupled idea: soft drop as the bar lands; avoid ticking counter sounds.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Your money, under your control — 4.5s
Reduce the interface to a simple privacy card: “Sem ligação a bancos. Sem anúncios. Os seus dados ficam sob o seu controlo.” Resolve to the OndeVai mark and “O seu dinheiro, explicado.” Hold the full line before the mark closes.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: gentle close; music fades under the final mark.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft final arrival; no voiceover.
Transition mood: slow crossfade to the final mark
